# Guided Onboarding / Role-Specific Guide Assembly

**Proposed Wiki category:** Workbench Handbook (`platform_handbook`)
**Proposed slug:** `guided-onboarding-role-specific-guide-assembly-workbench-method`
**Proposed status:** Draft -> human review -> approved
**Audience:** HR, people managers, and anyone assembling a role-specific onboarding path

## What it is

Guided Onboarding / Role-Specific Guide Assembly is a Workbench method for assembling a
role-specific onboarding guide from policies and processes that are already approved. It is a
compilation method, not a policy-writing one: it decides which existing material applies to a given
role and in what order, rather than authoring anything new.

## Why it matters

A generic "read the whole handbook" onboarding wastes a new hire's time and buries the handful of
things that actually apply to their role inside material that doesn't. A guide assembled per role,
with an honest gap list for anything that role's onboarding needs but doesn't yet have an approved
source, is more useful than either a giant handbook or an ad hoc list someone remembers to send.

## Requirements

**Required:** a target role definition; approved policy/process sources that cover that role.

**Optional:** manager notes; team-specific supplementary documents.

**Git required:** No.

## Method

Classify which approved policies and processes apply to the target role, sequence them into a
guided reading/task path (what to read or do first, second, third), and assemble the result as a
single artifact. Flag any part of that role's onboarding that has no corresponding approved source
yet, rather than inventing placeholder guidance to fill the gap.

## Deliverables

An Onboarding Guide artifact, specific to the role; a gap list naming anything the guide had to skip
because no approved source existed for it.

## Boundary

A curated compilation of existing approved material, not new policy authored on the fly. HR
approves the assembled guide before it's used with an actual new hire.
