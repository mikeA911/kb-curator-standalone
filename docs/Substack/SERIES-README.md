## Suggested cadence

Publish every two or three days rather than releasing the full set at once. Each article stands alone, while the sequence moves from visible AI transparency through context, interaction design, authority, privacy, deployment, and model operations.

## Before publishing

- Paste the title and subtitle into Substack's separate fields.
- Paste the article body beginning with the opening paragraph.
- Remove the Suggested Substack note from the article body; publish it separately as a Note when sharing the article.
- Preview both the web and email layouts.
- Confirm every external link.
- Add a suitable cover image only when it adds meaning; do not delay publication merely to decorate the post.
- Recheck time-sensitive technical statements immediately before publication.

## Editorial position

The articles are deliberately independent and non-promotional. They may be republished on an organisational blog, subject to its editorial policy, but should not be rewritten to imply that one product or framework automatically establishes trustworthy AI or compliance.
